# S-3 · Authorize payments in the order's currency

Epic: EPIC-RQ6 · Multi-currency checkout
Repo: payments-service
Team: Payments
Depends on: S-2
Risk: high

As Checkout, we want payments to be authorized in the currency of the order, so that shoppers are charged exactly the
amount they saw.

Acceptance criteria:
- An authorization request carries the amount in minor units together with its ISO-4217 currency code.
- A currency the payment provider does not support is declined with a typed reason, never converted silently.
- `PaymentCaptured` keeps its existing fields; any new field is additive.
