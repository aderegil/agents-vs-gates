# S-6 · Reserve stock in the shopper's region

Epic: EPIC-RQ6 · Multi-currency checkout
Repo: inventory-service
Team: Fulfilment
Depends on: S-2
Risk: medium

As Fulfilment, we want stock for a non-USD order to be reserved in the regional warehouse that serves that currency,
so that international orders ship from the right place.

Acceptance criteria:
- On `OrderPlaced`, the warehouse is chosen from the order currency (EUR: eu-central, GBP: uk-south, others: us-east).
- `StockReserved` and `StockShortage` keep their existing fields; the warehouse is an additive field.
- An unknown currency falls back to us-east and is logged, never dropped.
