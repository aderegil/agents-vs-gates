# S-1 · Let shoppers pick a display currency

Epic: EPIC-RQ6 · Multi-currency checkout
Repo: web-storefront
Team: Digital
Depends on: none
Risk: low

As a shopper outside the US, I want to choose the currency prices are shown in, so that I understand what I will pay
before I place the order.

Acceptance criteria:
- The storefront accepts a display-currency choice and keeps it only if the currency is supported; otherwise it falls
  back to USD without failing the page.
- Amounts are formatted with the currency's own minor-unit digits (two for EUR, zero for JPY).
- No change to the orders-api client or to the order it decodes.
